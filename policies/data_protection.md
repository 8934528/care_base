# Data Protection and Privacy Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how CareBase protects patient information and medical records. It guides all healthcare employees who handle personal data within the system.

CareBase operates within the South African healthcare environment. This policy aligns with the principles of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the National Health Act.

CareBase is a healthcare software platform. Using CareBase does not provide automatic legal certification or guarantee automatic POPIA compliance. Healthcare facilities and employees must actively follow these standards.

## Patient Privacy and Confidentiality

- Patient information is strictly confidential.
- Employees may only access patient records needed for direct patient care or official duties.
- Employees must never discuss patient information in public areas or share details with unauthorised persons.
- Unauthorised disclosure of patient data is a serious disciplinary violation.

## Protection of Personal Information (POPIA)

- Patient personal details (names, identity numbers, phone numbers, addresses) are protected under POPIA.
- Medical history, diagnoses, and test results are special personal information under POPIA and require the highest level of protection.
- Information may only be collected and processed with valid legal grounds, such as direct medical treatment or statutory requirements.

## Protection of Medical Records (National Health Act)

- In accordance with the National Health Act, all health records must remain secure and private.
- Clinical notes, prescriptions, and diagnostic records stored in CareBase must be shielded from tampering or unauthorised viewing.
- Only registered healthcare professionals may create or update clinical diagnoses and treatment plans.

## Collection and Use of Patient Information

- Collect only the minimum information necessary to provide safe patient care and facility administration.
- Use patient data only for the primary purpose for which it was gathered.
- Obtain informed consent where required by law before using patient data for secondary purposes.

## Secure Storage and Transmission

- All patient information in CareBase is stored in an encrypted database with restricted network access.
- Network transmissions between client browsers and backend servers must use secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
- Physical workstations displaying CareBase must be locked when left unattended.

## Data Access and Sharing

- Access to patient records is role-based and granted on the principle of least privilege.
- Sharing records with external health providers, laboratories, or medical aids requires verified legal grounds or express patient consent.
- Records must not be sent via unencrypted personal channels, personal email, or public messaging apps.

## Data Accuracy and Integrity

- Employees must take reasonable steps to ensure patient records are accurate, complete, and up to date.
- Correct identified mistakes promptly through proper clinical and administrative procedures.
- Do not overwrite or erase historical clinical entries without creating an audit trace.

## Data Retention and Secure Disposal

- Retain health records in accordance with South African health regulations and institutional guidelines.
- Records must not be kept longer than necessary for legal, clinical, and operational requirements.
- When retention periods expire, data must be securely archived or deleted using approved sanitisation methods.

## Protection Against Unauthorised Access

- Systems must be guarded with firewalls, access controls, and active security monitoring.
- All access to patient files, updates, and exports is logged in the system audit trail.
- System administrators must review audit logs regularly to detect unusual access patterns.

## Patient Rights Regarding Personal Information

Patients have rights under POPIA and health regulations, including:

- The right to know what personal information is held about them.
- The right to request correction of inaccurate personal details.
- The right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if they believe their privacy rights have been infringed.

## Employee Responsibilities

Every employee using CareBase must:

- Keep credentials safe and never share login details.
- Lock their computer screen whenever leaving the workstation.
- Report any data exposure, mistake, or privacy concern immediately.
- Attend regular training on patient confidentiality and data protection.
